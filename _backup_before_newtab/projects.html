Projects

1. Kabul

SAHAS is working with different government agencies to make sure the Hindu temples and Sikh Gurdwaras are maintained properly.

2. Delhi, India

SAHAS is committed to providing financial, immigration, and educational help to Afghan Hindu and Sikh refugees in Afghanistan.

During the COVID pandemic of 2021, SAHAS wired funds directly to the Afghan Hindu Sikh refugee family members who were locked down. SAHAS is now actively preparing the immigration paperwork for those who want to immigrate to the U.S., and is also working with other countries such as Canada and Australia which are accepting refugees.

USA-DV-2024 Visa

The U.S. Immigration Act of 1990 established the Diversity Visa (DV) program, which makes up to 55,000 immigrant visas available through random selection. The program allows those from Afghanistan to enter for a chance to apply for a U.S. immigrant visa. If your background is from Afghanistan, you are eligible to apply.

  • You do not need to be a citizen of Afghanistan to apply.
  • You do not need any documentation to apply.
  • It is totally free to apply.
  • The deadline to apply was November 5, 2022.

You only need the following information: your name, date of birth, address with PIN code, email address, cell phone number, and a digital photo.

To apply for the lottery draw, please use the following link: https://dvprogram.state.gov/application.aspx
